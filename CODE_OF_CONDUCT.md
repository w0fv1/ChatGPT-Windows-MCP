# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate professionally, discuss technical decisions in good faith, and keep reviews focused on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, discriminatory attacks, or sustained disruption of project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For conduct concerns, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub.
